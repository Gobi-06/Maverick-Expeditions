School & College Tour Packages from Tiruppur

Maverick Expeditions is a Tiruppur (Tirupur) travel agency that plans school and college tours, educational trips and industrial visits for batches of up to 150 students across Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka and Goa.

What is included

Every student package includes vetted buses, an escort who travels with the group, a coordinator per bus for larger batches, guides at heritage and wildlife sites, and permission letters and site coordination for industrial visits.

Popular student destinations

Ooty and Kodaikanal for hill-station trips, Munnar and Thekkady for nature and wildlife, Alleppey backwaters, Mysuru and Coorg, Hampi for history, and Goa for final-year batches. See all on our destinations page.

How to book

Tell us your batch size, dates and budget on the Plan Your Trip page or call +91 81108 49375. Most quotes go out the same day, and we hold room blocks well ahead of the season.

Frequently asked questions

Do you organise tours for schools and colleges from Tiruppur?

Yes. We plan educational tours, study tours and industrial visits for schools and colleges in Tiruppur, Coimbatore and Erode, from a single class to a batch of 150.

Are student tours safe?

Every group travels with an escort, vetted vehicles and a coordinator per bus for large batches, with stays chosen for group safety.

Can you arrange industrial visits?

Yes. We handle permission letters and site coordination for industrial visits alongside the sightseeing itinerary.
